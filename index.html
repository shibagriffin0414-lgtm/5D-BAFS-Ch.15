HKDSE Official Textbook Syllabus

Section 15.2: Need for Revaluation

Revaluation should be performed immediately upon:

  • A change in the profit and loss sharing ratio
  • Admission of partners
  • Withdrawal (retirement or death) of partners

Why share gain/loss immediately among old partners?

The change in net assets ($\text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities} = \text{Capital}$) arises while the old partnership is in operation. If revaluation is not done, when assets are eventually sold, new partners would share holding gains/losses under the new ratio without paying, while retiring partners would lose out without compensation.

Section 15.3: 4 Steps to Record Revaluation

  1. Record asset/liability changes: Increase in asset → Dr Asset; Decrease → Cr Asset. Increase in liability → Cr Liability; Decrease → Dr Liability.
  2. Post corresponding entries to the Revaluation account.
  3. Compute the overall gain or loss on revaluation.
  4. Make adjusting entries in old partners' capital accounts (NOT current accounts!) in their old profit and loss sharing ratio.
Offsetting Accumulated Depreciation: When a tangible non-current asset is revalued, transfer out its accumulated depreciation first:
Dr Accumulated depreciation: Asset  /  Cr Asset

Textbook Answering Tips: Revaluation Wordings ("to" vs "by")

Misunderstanding "to" and "by" leads to wrong answers!
Wording Used Textbook Example Accounting Implication
Percentage of original NBV ("to X%") "Equipment was to be revalued to 40% of the net book value." Revalued amount = $\text{NBV} \times 40\%$
Loss on revaluation = $\text{NBV} \times 60\%$
Percentage change ("by X%") "Net book value of equipment was to be decreased by 40%." Revalued amount = $\text{NBV} \times 60\%$
Loss on revaluation = $\text{NBV} \times 40\%$
Lump sum change ("by $X") "Equipment was to be revalued upwards by $15,000." Revalued amount = $\text{NBV} + \$15,000$
Gain on revaluation = $\$15,000$
Directly stated ("at $X") "Equipment was to be revalued at $15,000." Revalued amount = $\$15,000$
Gain on revaluation = $\$15,000 - \text{NBV}$

Section 15.4: Revaluation of Goodwill (Case A vs Case B)

Goodwill should be revalued together with other separable assets and liabilities. The adjusting entries depend on whether a goodwill account had been opened in the books of the old partnership:

Case A: A goodwill account HAD been opened

Treated just like any other non-current asset through the Revaluation account, and Goodwill stays on the new SOFP at its revalued amount:

Increase in goodwill:
Dr Goodwill  /  Cr Revaluation
Decrease in goodwill:
Dr Revaluation  /  Cr Goodwill
Case B: A goodwill account had NOT been opened

Goodwill is NOT entered in the Revaluation account and NO Goodwill account appears on the new SOFP. Adjust directly in partners' Capital accounts:

1. Credit old partners with Goodwill shared in old ratio.
2. Debit new/continuing partners with Goodwill shared in new ratio.

Section 15.5: Effect on Depreciation Charges

After revaluation, depreciation should be charged on the revalued amount of a tangible non-current asset over its remaining useful life (using the revised estimated useful life if adjusted).

Annual Depreciation = Revalued Amount ÷ Remaining Useful Life

Textbook Example (Exhibit 15.6): Machine bought 1 Jan 2018 for $100,000 (10-year life). On 1 Jan 2022 (4 years passed → 6 years left), revalued at $90,000. New annual depreciation = $90,000 ÷ 6 = $15,000.

Textbook Page 96 Official Summary

  • 1(i): Overall gain or loss on revaluation is shared immediately among old partners in the old profit and loss sharing ratio.
  • 1(ii): A revaluation account records gains and losses on revaluation of assets and liabilities.
  • 2: Assets and liabilities of the new partnership are shown at revalued amounts in the SOFP.
  • 3: Goodwill is revalued together with separable assets/liabilities; entries depend on whether a goodwill account has been opened or not.
  • 4: Depreciation after revaluation is charged on the revalued amount over its remaining useful life.
  • Retiring Partner Note (Ch 14 link): When a partner withdraws, transfer his Current Account balance to his Capital Account before settlement/loan transfer.
